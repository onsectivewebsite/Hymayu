Founder & Principal

Dr. Humayun Chaudhary

PhD (Taxation), MBA, LLM, CPA

Dr. Humayun Chaudhary is a distinguished professor, academic leader, and practicing accounting and tax professional with over two decades of combined experience in higher education, public policy, and professional practice.

He currently serves as Professor of Accounting and Taxation at Sheridan College's Pilon School of Business and Course Director at York University's School of Administrative Studies.

Dr. Chaudhary holds a Ph.D. in Accounting and Tax Policy from Osgoode Hall Law School, York University, complemented by a Master of Laws (LLM) in Business Law, an MBA in Accounting, and advanced management education from the Schulich School of Business.

CPA — Ontario CPA — USA CGMA — Chartered Global MA FCCA — UK (Fellow) Chartered Management Accountant — UK PhD — Osgoode Hall

As founder and principal of Indus Canada CPA, Dr. Chaudhary provides taxation, financial reporting, and strategic business advisory services to small and mid-sized enterprises — bringing a rare combination of academic authority and applied, client-first insight to every engagement.

Our Team

The people behind your numbers

Our in-house accounting team works directly with you through the full accounting cycle — bookkeeping, payroll, GST/HST compliance, and personal and corporate tax.

Tax & Accounting Associate

Parneet Kaur

Parneet Kaur is a Tax & Accounting Associate at Indus Canada CPA, where she supports entrepreneurs, professionals, and growing businesses with comprehensive accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and tax services.

Known for her attention to detail and commitment to excellence, Parneet works closely with clients to maintain accurate financial records, manage payroll obligations, prepare financial reports, ensure GST/HST compliance, and support personal and corporate tax filings. Her ability to combine technical precision with practical business insight helps clients navigate complex financial matters with confidence.

She plays an integral role in delivering the high standard of service that clients expect from Indus Canada CPA, assisting businesses throughout the full accounting cycle while helping them strengthen internal financial processes and meet regulatory requirements efficiently.

Currently pursuing an Honours Bachelor of Business Administration in Accounting at Sheridan College and working toward her CPA designation, Parneet continues to expand her expertise in Canadian taxation, financial reporting, and accounting practices. Her dedication to professional growth and client service reflects the firm's commitment to providing trusted, forward-thinking financial guidance.

As part of the Indus Canada CPA team, Parneet contributes to delivering sophisticated accounting and tax solutions that help clients build stronger businesses, achieve compliance with confidence, and create a solid foundation for long-term success.
