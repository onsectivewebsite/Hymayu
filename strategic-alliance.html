About Naveed Anwar, MA, LLM · Barrister & Solicitor

Strategic Tax Litigation & Advocacy, Tailored To Every Client

"Professionalism, precision, and a commitment to achieving practical, effective results."

Naveed Anwar is a skilled and results-driven lawyer licensed in Ontario and Alberta, with a focused practice in tax litigation, tax disputes, and tax appeals. He represents individuals, corporations, and businesses in complex matters involving the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), provincial tax authorities, and administrative tribunals, providing strategic legal solutions tailored to each client’s circumstances.

With extensive experience handling tax audits, reassessments, collections disputes, penalties, interest relief applications, and taxpayer appeals, Naveed advocates effectively for clients at every stage of the dispute resolution process. He represents clients before the Tax Court of Canada, Federal Court, CRA Appeals Division, and other administrative bodies, ensuring their rights and interests are protected through detailed preparation and strong advocacy.

His practice focuses exclusively on the legal side of tax matters, including litigation strategy, dispute resolution, negotiations with tax authorities, and representation in complex appeals and enforcement proceedings. To further strengthen client outcomes, Naveed works in strategic partnership with Indus Canada CPA, leveraging their accounting and tax expertise to support complex financial analysis, compliance review, and evidentiary matters where required.

A member of the Law Society of Ontario, Naveed brings a multicultural perspective to his practice and is fluent in English, Punjabi, Hindi, and Urdu, allowing him to provide accessible and effective legal representation to a diverse clientele. His approach is defined by professionalism, precision, and a commitment to achieving practical and effective results for his clients.

Whether challenging CRA reassessments, defending taxpayers in disputes, negotiating resolutions, or representing clients in tax appeals and litigation, Naveed upholds the highest standards of legal advocacy and client service.

About Erum Farooqi · Financial & Wealth Advisor

Strategic Wealth Planning For Long-Term Growth

"Practical, compliant, and results-driven financial guidance — tailored to every client."

Erum Farooqi is a trusted Financial and Wealth Advisor who works with individuals, families, and business owners to build strategic wealth management plans focused on long-term financial growth, asset protection, and tax efficiency. Her approach is centered on creating legally compliant financial structures that help clients preserve and grow wealth while significantly reducing unnecessary tax exposure.

Erum provides tailored guidance in wealth management, retirement planning, investment strategies, estate planning, and insurance solutions for both individuals and businesses. She works closely with clients to design integrated financial plans that align with their goals, risk tolerance, family needs, and business objectives.

With the strong accounting and tax expertise backing of Indus Canada CPA, Erum helps clients implement advanced financial strategies such as HELOC structuring, leveraged investing, and the Smith Manoeuvre, ensuring every strategy is properly structured, tax-efficient, and compliant with Canadian tax laws. This collaborative approach allows clients to benefit from coordinated financial planning, tax strategy, and wealth preservation under one trusted advisory team.

Whether assisting business owners with corporate wealth planning, helping families build tax-efficient investment portfolios, or structuring insurance and financing solutions to maximize long-term financial security, Erum is committed to delivering practical, compliant, and results-driven financial guidance tailored to every client.

About Saket Seth, LLB · Barrister & Solicitor

Preserving Relationships Are The Basis Of Success

"Personalized attention, transparent guidance, and strong representation."

Saket Seth, LLB, is a dedicated Barrister & Solicitor committed to delivering practical, timely, and client-centered legal solutions. With a strong foundation in Canadian law and experience across litigation, legal advisory, and real estate matters, he brings a strategic, detail-oriented, and professional approach to every client matter.

Saket works collaboratively with legal and financial professionals, including Naveed Anwar and the team at Indus Canada CPA, on select tax litigation, CRA dispute, and regulatory matters where coordinated legal and financial strategy is essential. His collaborative approach helps ensure clients receive comprehensive support when navigating complex legal and tax-related challenges.

In addition to his collaborative work on tax-related matters, Saket independently maintains a strong real estate practice, assisting individuals, investors, and businesses with residential and commercial transactions, agreements, closings, and property-related legal matters. He is committed to ensuring transactions are completed efficiently, accurately, and in the best interests of his clients.

Saket believes every client deserves personalized attention, transparent guidance, and strong representation. His practice reflects professionalism, responsiveness, and a commitment to helping clients navigate legal matters with confidence and clarity.
