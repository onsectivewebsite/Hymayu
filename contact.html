FAQ

Common questions

Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes. The first 30-minute consultation is completely free with no obligation. We use it to understand your situation and tell you whether — and how — we can help.

Do you work with clients outside the GTA?

Absolutely. We serve clients across Canada (and many non-resident clients abroad) using secure document portals, video calls, and e-signatures. You don't need to be in our office.

How fast can you take on a new client?

During tax season (Feb-Apr) we onboard within 3 business days. The rest of the year, same-day onboarding is typical.

How are your fees structured?

Fixed-fee engagements wherever possible. You'll know the cost before we begin work — no surprise invoices. Personal returns from $80, monthly bookkeeping from $250, corporate returns from $700.

Is my information secure?

Yes. We use bank-grade encrypted document portals, MFA on all professional software, and follow CPA Ontario's strict confidentiality requirements. We never email tax documents in plain text.
