Our Story

Founder & Principal

Dr. Humayun Chaudhary

PhD (Taxation), MBA, LLM, CPA

Dr. Humayun Chaudhary is a distinguished professor, academic leader, and practicing accounting and tax professional with over two decades of combined experience in higher education, public policy, and professional practice.

He currently serves as Professor of Accounting and Taxation at Sheridan College's Pilon School of Business and Course Director at York University's School of Administrative Studies.

Dr. Chaudhary holds a Ph.D. in Accounting and Tax Policy from Osgoode Hall Law School, York University, complemented by a Master of Laws (LLM) in Business Law, an MBA in Accounting, and advanced management education from the Schulich School of Business.

His professional credentials are exceptionally broad and globally recognized:

CPA — Ontario CPA — USA CGMA — Chartered Global MA FCCA — UK (Fellow) Chartered Management Accountant — UK PhD — Osgoode Hall

In parallel with his academic career, Dr. Chaudhary is the founder and principal of Indus Canada CPA, providing taxation, financial reporting, and strategic business advisory services to small and mid-sized enterprises. Earlier in his career, he served as Senior Financial Analyst with Ontario's Ministry of Health and Long-Term Care, contributing to multi-million-dollar capital planning initiatives and supporting evidence-based public-sector decision-making.

Through his integrated work as an educator, examiner, researcher, practitioner, and public servant, Dr. Chaudhary brings a rare combination of academic rigour, professional authority, and applied insight — making him a trusted voice in accounting education, taxation policy, and business decision-making.
