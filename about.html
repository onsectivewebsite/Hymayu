Our Story

Every successful firm begins with a simple idea: people deserve someone they can trust when it comes to their finances.

After spending more than two decades working in accounting, finance, taxation, and management consulting, Dr. Humayun Chaudhary noticed the same pattern over and over again. Business owners were passionate about growing their companies, families wanted peace of mind with their taxes, and entrepreneurs had big dreams—but many struggled to navigate the increasingly complex world of accounting and tax compliance. Too often, they received generic advice instead of personalized guidance.

While teaching taxation at the Pilon School of Business for over ten years and serving as a course director at York University for nearly two decades, Humayun had the privilege of educating the next generation of accountants and business leaders. The classroom kept him connected to the latest developments in tax policy, while his consulting work allowed him to see firsthand the real challenges faced by Canadian businesses and individuals.

Armed with an exceptional academic background—including a Ph.D. in Tax Policy, multiple master’s degrees, and professional accounting designations from Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom—Humayun realized that knowledge alone wasn’t enough. What truly made a difference was using that expertise to simplify complicated problems and help clients make confident financial decisions.

That vision led to the creation of Indus Canada CPA Professional Corporation.

The firm’s mission was straightforward: provide honest, practical, and professional accounting services that help people solve real financial challenges. Whether supporting a new entrepreneur setting up their first corporation, helping a family navigate tax planning, assisting businesses with bookkeeping and financial reporting, or representing clients through complex tax matters, Indus Canada CPA was built on the belief that every client deserves clear advice, careful attention, and long-term support.

Today, Indus Canada CPA combines academic excellence with decades of practical experience to serve individuals, professionals, and businesses across Canada. Under Humayun’s leadership, the firm continues to focus on building lasting relationships, delivering personalized solutions, and helping clients achieve financial clarity and peace of mind.

For Humayun, accounting has never been just about numbers. It has always been about helping people solve problems, make better decisions, and build a stronger future.
